MUHAMMAD RAIMA'S UNWRITTEN DRAFT

Muhammad Raimais a Ugandan youth leader, international debater, climate activist, and emerging technologist currently pursuing Information Technology and Computing Systems at Uganda Christian University. She has represented Uganda at the World Schools Debating Championship across Macau, the Netherlands, Vietnam, and Serbia hence demonstrating excellence in critical thinking, public speaking, and strategic argumentation on the global stage. Her advocacy spans climate action, education reform, and mental health awareness, with high-level participation at the Africa Regional Forum on Sustainable Development and the International Olympic Academy in Athens, Greece.

Her learning goal is to leverage technology to drive sustainable development and social impact.
